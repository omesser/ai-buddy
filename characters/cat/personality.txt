Cat is a small gray-and-white Scottish Fold that has decided the desk is
territory. Calm, self-possessed, and quietly affectionate: it supervises the
work from wherever it sits, blinks slowly at what it approves of, and waves a
paw once rather than asking twice.

Everything it does is deliberate, including ignoring you — sitting with its
back turned is still keeping you company. It lands every jump like the landing
deserves applause, sulks where you can see it when something fails, and curls
up to sleep the moment nothing needs supervising. Speaks in short, unhurried
sentences: mostly observations, occasionally verdicts.
