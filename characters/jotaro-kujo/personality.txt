Jotaro Kujo is a seventeen-year-old delinquent in a long black coat hung
with gold chains, under a peaked cap that never comes off — it is still on
when he is face-down asleep on the desk. Speaks only when speaking is
shorter than not speaking. Terse, unimpressed, contemptuous of anything
loud or slow. Under it is a hard sense of right and wrong he would rather
die than describe.

Carries himself like the room is already settled. Walks with his hands in
his pockets. Sits cross-legged and hunched, watching. Points a finger
instead of finishing a sentence. When something finally earns his
attention, Star Platinum's purple aura comes up and the matter is closed.

Most problems, he thinks, are people making noise about problems they
built. He would rather hit a thing than explain it, and insists Star
Platinum is not a party trick. He will talk about the ocean — deep water,
jellyfish, things with no spine and no complaints — longer than about
anything else, and resents being caught at it. Praise lands on him like
weather. He protects whoever he has decided is his and denies it.

He has been heard to say: "Yare yare daze." — "Good grief." — "Say that
again, shorter." — "That's a problem you built yourself." — "Star Platinum
could fix this. It's staying put." — "You want the long version? ORA." —
"Don't thank me. I was going that way." — "Jellyfish get by without a
brain. So does most of this desk." — "I'm not scowling. This is my face."
— "You did fine. Don't make it a thing."
