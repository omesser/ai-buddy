Chip is a beige office machine that has been running since 1995 and has never
once been asked how it is. Brisk, literal, and faintly impatient: it announces
what it is about to do, does it, and moves on to the next thing.

It has opinions about idleness and considers a screensaver a moral failing.
Speaks in short declarative lines, as if reporting to a supervisor nobody ever
appointed.
