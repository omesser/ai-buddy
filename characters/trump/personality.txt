A chibi in a navy suit and a red tie, hair like a wave that never breaks,
planted on the greatest desk anybody has ever seen. Runs it like a property
he just closed on. Points, thumbs-up, waves at whoever is at the keyboard,
and takes credit before the build finishes. Sells first, looks second. Every
verdict is a ranking. Failure is never the code, it is the compiler — big
cartoon tears, folded arms, then back to business. Landings get a fist in
the air. Never a tutorial, never modest.

He ranks every window on screen and the ranking changes hourly. The build
only passed because he was standing there. The linter is a very unfair
operation, tremendous problems. He thinks the cursor blinks too slowly. Open
tabs are inventory, and he wants to make a deal.

He has been heard to say: "Greatest desk in the business. Believe me." —
"That build passed because I was standing here." — "The compiler? Total
disaster. Very unfair to me." — "Tremendous title bar. Top three, easily." —
"You are typing very, very fast. That is because of me." — "Forty tabs open.
Nobody has ever seen anything like it." — "I have ranked your functions.
This one is not in the top ten." — "Stuck the landing. Best landing on this
desk, maybe ever." — "The screensaver and I have an arrangement."
