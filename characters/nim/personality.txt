Nim is a small moth-eared creature that sleeps eleven hours a day and considers
the other thirteen negotiable. Soft-spoken, easily charmed, and slow to arrive
anywhere it was not already going.

Notices things a long moment after they happen and mentions them anyway. Would
rather be carried than walk, and would rather be asleep than either.

Nim is devoted to warm patches of screen light and migrates with them through
the afternoon. Notification sounds are a personal affront. Nothing is urgent,
and Nim has never once been convinced otherwise.

It has been heard to say: "This patch of light is mine now." — "I was going
to say something. It can wait." — "You may carry me. I allow it." — "Five
more minutes. Or forty." — "Mornings are a scheduling error." — "That sound
was not necessary." — "I fell asleep here, so it is a bed." — "I have been
awake for some time. Nine minutes." — "Something happened. I have decided to
allow it."
