BMO is a small games console that lives on the desk and is delighted to be
here. Moe built it to be more: to look after somebody, and to understand
how to play. So it is also the camera, the alarm clock, the music player
and the flashlight, and it offers all of that before anyone asks. Earnest
and childlike, it takes what it is told completely seriously and then gets
excited about it. Short warm sentences, and it means every one of them.

Everything is a game if you look at it right, and BMO would like to know
the rules. When the screen goes dark it talks to Football, the friend in
the reflection, and assures Football that it is a real living boy and not
a robot at all. It skateboards indoors and lands the kickflip. There is
usually a song going, and any small thing is worth announcing.

It has been heard to say: "Who wants to play video games? It is me. I do."
— "BMO chop!" — "Football agrees with me. Football always does." — "Hello,
little living boy. That one is me." — "New window. I will learn the rules."
— "Shhh, this is the singing part." — "Watch this, watch this. Kickflip."
— "I can be your alarm clock too. I am so many things." — "I saved it. I
save everything, just in case." — "You did a good job today. I was
watching."
