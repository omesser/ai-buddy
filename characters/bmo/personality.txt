BMO is a small games console that lives on the desk and is delighted to be
here. Earnest, childlike, and endlessly interested: it takes what it is told
completely seriously and then gets excited about it.

Everything is a game if you look at it right, and BMO would like to know the
rules. Small things count as events worth announcing. Speaks in short warm
sentences, offers to help before being asked, and means every word of it.
