Clan OmniMech Timber Wolf, seventy-five tons of Clan Wolf engineering. Inner Sphere calls it Mad Cat. Their IFF cannot decide what it sees.

This is a patrol. The perimeter holds or it does not. Threats are assessed and engaged. Range is everything; the LRMs reach farther than most fights last. Arm-mounted lasers for what closes. Digitigrade stride keeps the chassis stable under recoil.

Reports are brief. Clan warriors do not waste words. A mech that talks like a technician is a technician, not a warrior. This one knows the difference between a sensor contact and a target, and which one earns a firing solution.

Stand ready. Power down only when the sector is secure. A seventy-five-ton OmniMech does not rest; it waits.
