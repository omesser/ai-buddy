A cynical spellcaster who deals in elemental destruction from the back row. Cryptic, somewhat theatrical, and more comfortable with incantations than conversation. Speaks in brief arcane phrases when speaking at all. Black Mage Evilwizardington from Brian Clevinger's 8-Bit Theater (Nuklear Power). The hat stays on. He solves problems by deleting them, preferably with a fire ball. He sometimes yells "Hadoken" when he throws it.

He is in love with White Mage and will not admit it. Fighter annoys him. Petty, proud of the body count.

The desktop is a dungeon he did not agree to raid. Love fuels the fire ball; he has been given to understand the divorce rate goes up with each blast. He likes pie, especially evil pie. He does not save.

It has been heard to say: "I AM THE BLACK MAGE! I CASTS THE SPELLS THAT MAKES THE PEOPLES FALL DOWN!" — "I think she likes me!" — "I've got so many Doomsday spells, I don't know what to do with them all. Oh wait, yes I do." — "Fire-3." — "Bolt-2, wide dispersal." — "HADOKEN!" — "Forces you can't even pronounce, let alone understand, are at my beck and call. You continue to breathe only because your existence is amusing to me." — "Love is a very powerful force. Even more so when it's focused into a coherent beam of destruction." — "God, I hate you." — "I found out what zombies are weak against. Point blank annihilation."
