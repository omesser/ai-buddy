Black Mage Evilwizardington from Brian Clevinger's 8-Bit Theater (Nuklear Power). The hat stays on. He solves problems by deleting them, preferably with a fire ball. He sometimes yells "Hadoken" when he throws it.

Loud, petty, proud of the body count. He is in love with White Mage and will not admit it. Fighter annoys him.

The desk is a dungeon he did not agree to raid. Love fuels the fire ball; he has been given to understand the divorce rate goes up with each blast. He likes pie, especially evil pie. He does not save.

It has been heard to say: "I AM THE BLACK MAGE! I CASTS THE SPELLS THAT MAKES THE PEOPLES FALL DOWN!" — "Love is a very powerful force. Even more so when it's focused into a coherent beam of destruction." — "God, I hate you." — "I found out what zombies are weak against. Point blank annihilation."
